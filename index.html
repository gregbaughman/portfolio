About Me

I am a pressure testing manufacturing engineer at Emerson, where I design fixtures and test setups for many different products and assemblies, as well as create testing systems and tools for hydrostatic and cryogenic applications.

I graduated from Iowa State University with a Bachelor of Science in Aerospace Engineering. My journey at ISU was enriched by my involvement in the Greek community through my fraternity, as well as my active participation in the Iowa State Weight Club and SALT ministries. I also served as a peer mentor in the Aerospace Department, where I had the privilege of guiding and supporting the freshman class.

In my free time, I find great joy in building and flying radio-controlled FPV systems, Woodworking, as well as exploring the outdoors with my wife and dog. I am driven by my passion for controls engineering and learning the intricacies of different systems and how they interact with each other. I have and will always love the design and build process (as well as the inevitable revise and rebuild). I am excited to see where my career leads me and contribute to projects with a global imapact.
